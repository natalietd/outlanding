# Privacy policy: Outlanding

Last updated: 20 November 2026

Outlanding is a Claude plugin made of instructions and reference files. It contains no code that runs on its own, no analytics and no telemetry.

## What the plugin collects

Nothing. The plugin doesn't collect, store or send personal data, and the author receives no information about you or how you use it.

## What happens when you use it

- **Your inputs stay in your Claude conversation.** Your brief, product facts and screenshots are handled by Claude under [Anthropic's privacy policy](https://www.anthropic.com/legal/privacy).
- **Web pages it opens.** To study competitors and check your page, Claude opens public web pages with the browser tools in your session: competitor sites, the URLs you give it and your own page. Those sites see an ordinary page visit.
- **Files it creates.** Screenshots, notes and the landing page are saved only where you choose, such as a `research/` folder in your project. They never go to the author.
